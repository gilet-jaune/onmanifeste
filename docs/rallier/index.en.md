# Why join us?

The yellow vests' and high-school students' causes (purchasing power, pensions, public services, democracy) concern far more people than you might think. Pick a profile: you will find concrete, sourced stakes, common objections and a possible first step. Useful to make up your mind, to convince someone close to you or for a debate.
