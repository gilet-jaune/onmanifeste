# Environment and health: what the decisions say

Climate, glyphosate, neonicotinoids: this timeline gathers dated decisions (courts, the Constitutional Council, European institutions) and what NGOs and the government say about them. It does not settle scientific debates: it shows **where things moved forward, backward or changed course**, and who decided what.

    Each entry links to its sources. When readings differ (NGOs, ministry), both are given and attributed. A mistake, something missing? [Report it]({{ repositories.docs.url }}/issues/new/choose).

## Pesticides: "authorised, banned, then authorised again"

- **Neonicotinoids in France.** On 7 August 2025 the Constitutional Council strikes down the exemption from the ban provided for by the Duplomb law, in the name of the right to live in a balanced environment that respects health (Article 1 of the Environmental Charter).[^cc-duplomb-2025] On 14 August 2026 it upholds, with reservations, an agricultural emergency law which, according to Reporterre, reintroduces acetamiprid and flupyradifurone on a derogatory basis.[^cc-2026-914-dc-urgence-agricole][^reporterre-cc-urgence-agricole-2026] These are two different texts: in its 2026 reasoning, the Council notes that the exemption, granted by ANSES, requires a serious threat, no alternatives and a research plan, that it cannot be granted where there are significant risks to health or the environment, and that these provisions are repealed after three years.
- **Neonicotinoids in Europe.** The Court of Justice of the European Union ruled in January 2023 that a Member State cannot authorise treated seeds when a European regulation has expressly banned them.[^eurlex-arret-c-162-21-neonicotinoides]
- **Glyphosate.** With no qualified majority, the European Commission renewed its approval for ten years (16 December 2023 - 15 December 2033), for herbicide uses only.[^eurlex-glyphosate-2023-2660] France abstained; Foodwatch and Générations futures denounce a betrayal of the promise made in 2017 by the President.[^france24-glyphosate-renouvellement-2023]
- **Risk assessment.** In the "Justice pour le vivant" case, the Paris Administrative Court recognised the State's culpable failure in June 2023.[^actu-environnement-ta-paris-2023-jpv] On 3 September 2025 the Paris Administrative Court of Appeal finds ANSES at fault for not systematically assessing products in light of the latest scientific knowledge, and orders a review of authorisations already granted within twenty-four months; it dismisses, however, the fault linked to the Ecophyto plans, whose numerical targets are not binding. An appeal on points of law was registered on 23 October 2025.[^caa-paris-2025-jpv] The NGO coalition also criticises the replacement, in 2024, of the Ecophyto plan's monitoring indicator with one it considers less protective: this is an NGO position.[^naat-justice-pour-le-vivant-jugement]

!!! warning "What these decisions do not establish"

    According to the sources read, the decisions cited concern the legality of texts and ecological damage, not the link between a product and a person's illness. On glyphosate, scientific opinions diverge: the International Agency for Research on Cancer classed it as "probably carcinogenic" in 2015, whereas the European Food Safety Authority found no "critical area of concern" in 2023, while acknowledging data gaps.[^france24-glyphosate-renouvellement-2023]

## Climate: the Affaire du Siècle

- **2021.** After a case filed in March 2019 by four associations, the Paris Administrative Court recognises on 3 February ecological damage and the State's liability for its partial failure.[^ta-paris-affaire-du-siecle-2021-02] On 14 October it orders the repair of an overrun of the first carbon budget by 31 December 2022 at the latest.[^ta-paris-affaire-du-siecle-2021-10]
- **2023: two readings.** On 22 December the court rejects the request for a penalty payment. According to Notre Affaire à Tous's note, it rules that the 2021 judgment is not fully executed but refuses any further measure;[^naat-fiche-affaire-du-siecle-2023] Greenpeace concludes that the State "did not execute the judgment",[^greenpeace-affaire-du-siecle-2023] whereas the Ministry of Ecological Transition sees confirmation of repair within the deadlines.[^ecologie-affaire-du-siecle-2023]
