# Environment and health: what the decisions say

Climate, glyphosate, neonicotinoids: this timeline gathers dated decisions (courts, the Constitutional Council, European institutions) and what NGOs and the government say about them. It does not settle scientific debates: it shows **where things moved forward, backward or changed course**, and who decided what.

!!! info "Our rules"

## Pesticides: two texts not to be confused

The pesticide decisions (Constitutional Council, Court of Justice of the European Union, administrative courts, glyphosate) are on the timeline above and, with the others, in the [historical landmarks](../histoire/index.md#kind=environnement-sante). Two points the cards do not carry:

- **Neonicotinoids: two different texts.** On 7 August 2025 the Constitutional Council strikes down the exemption from the ban provided for by the Duplomb law, in the name of the right to live in a balanced environment that respects health (Article 1 of the Environmental Charter).[^cc-duplomb-2025] On 14 August 2026 it upholds, with reservations, an agricultural emergency law which, according to Reporterre, reintroduces acetamiprid and flupyradifurone on a derogatory basis.[^cc-2026-914-dc-urgence-agricole][^reporterre-cc-urgence-agricole-2026] In its 2026 reasoning, the Council notes that the exemption, granted by ANSES, requires a serious threat, no alternatives and a research plan, that it cannot be granted where there are significant risks to health or the environment, and that these provisions are repealed after three years.
- **Ecophyto plan.** The NGO coalition criticises the replacement, in 2024, of the Ecophyto plan's monitoring indicator with one it considers less protective: this is an NGO position.[^naat-justice-pour-le-vivant-jugement] The administrative court of appeal, for its part, dismissed the fault linked to that plan, whose numerical targets are not binding.[^caa-paris-2025-jpv]

!!! warning "What these decisions do not establish"

    According to the sources read, the decisions cited concern the legality of texts and ecological damage, not the link between a product and a person's illness. On glyphosate, scientific opinions diverge: the International Agency for Research on Cancer classed it as "probably carcinogenic" in 2015, whereas the European Food Safety Authority found no "critical area of concern" in 2023, while acknowledging data gaps.[^france24-glyphosate-renouvellement-2023]

## Climate: the Affaire du Siècle

The 3 February 2021 judgment is on the timeline; the [October 2021 order to repair](../histoire/index.md#evt-2021-affaire-du-siecle-injonction-de-reparer) and the [December 2023 enforcement judgment](../histoire/index.md#evt-2023-affaire-du-siecle-jugement-d-execution) are in the historical landmarks. On 22 December 2023 the court rejects the request for a penalty payment; three actors draw different readings from it, attributed below:[^naat-fiche-affaire-du-siecle-2023]

| Who | Reading of the decision |
| --- | --- |
| Notre Affaire à Tous | The court rules that the 2021 judgment is not fully executed, but refuses any further measure.[^naat-fiche-affaire-du-siecle-2023] |
| Greenpeace | The State "did not execute the judgment".[^greenpeace-affaire-du-siecle-2023] |
| Ministry of Ecological Transition | The decision confirms repair within the deadlines.[^ecologie-affaire-du-siecle-2023] |
