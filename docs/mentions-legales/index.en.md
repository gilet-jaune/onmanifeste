# Legal notice and privacy

## Publisher and publishing approach

This site is a collaborative, independent and **volunteer** documentation project, developed under pseudonymity by the collective behind the [{{ repositories.docs.name }}]({{ repositories.docs.url }}) repository.

Under French law (the LCEN digital economy act), a non-professional publisher's exemption from publishing civil identity details theoretically requires providing identification elements to the host.[^lcen][^anonymat] Because GitHub Pages does not collect civil identity records for free accounts and public repositories, this project embraces **pseudonymous publishing**, which is essential to the safety and freedom of expression of its contributors.

For any legitimate request (right of removal, notice of inaccurate content or infringement of a third party's rights):

- **Public feedback / corrections**: [issue forms]({{ repositories.docs.url }}/issues/new/choose)
- **Confidential requests (personal data, removal)**: [private report]({{ repositories.docs.url }}/security/advisories/new)

## Funding and independence

This site receives **no outside funding**: no grant, no advertising, no donation, no commercial partnership. The running costs, mainly the domain name, are paid by the publisher (the hosting itself is provided by GitHub Pages, see above). Nobody earns an income from the site, and it depends on no party, union, media outlet or company.

## Personal data and privacy

- **No analytics, no trackers, no accounts.** The site uses neither advertising cookies nor statistics tools.
- **No third-party requests** from your browser: fonts and assets are served by the site itself.
- **Local storage only**: the offline copy (service worker cache) and your choices (install banner hidden, offline mode turned off) stay in your browser. The [Settings](../parametres.md) page lets you manage them and delete the offline copy.
- **Host logs**: like any server, GitHub Pages logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes.[^github-pages] If this exposes you, browse the site with [Tor Browser](https://www.torproject.org/) or a VPN, or install it once and then use it offline.

## Content and liability

Information is general and does not replace a lawyer or a medical professional. Data and texts were first **researched and sourced with the help of an artificial intelligence**, and are being reviewed by humans step by step. Every statement is sourced and dated, and the check indicator (`check`) says who checked it: nobody yet (not reviewed by a human), volunteer, lawyer, health professional or historian. Content not yet reviewed by a professional or not confirmed carries a badge. See the [content policy]({{ repositories.docs.url }}/blob/main/MODERATION.md) (in French).

## Why an AI?

The details (what the AI does, what it does not do, its limits) are on the page [How we use AI](../ia/index.md).

The aim is not to produce new information, but to **gather and make accessible, including offline**, useful information already published by public sources (laws, associations, public services, media). The AI was used to find and summarise them; every claim points to its source so that you can check it.

This site aims to be **complete** and usable **offline**, by gathering content that already exists and pointing to it (laws, associations, media, public services). For a volunteer, anonymous project, an AI was used to **research, source and write** these summaries, and also to **build the site** (code, tooling, tests) quickly, on **free, reusable technologies** (MkDocs, Material for MkDocs): anyone can copy the site and adapt it.

AI has a cost (energy, resources) and several causes defended here oppose it: we acknowledge that. What it produces has not yet been reviewed by humans. Each piece of information therefore carries its source, its date and a badge saying who checked it. AI does not replace review by humans, nor the expertise of collectives, lawyers and health professionals: this is why each entry says whether it has been reviewed, and by whom. We are looking for reviewers, and we point to the collectives whose work this is. If you can review a guide, see [Contribute](../contribuer/index.md).
