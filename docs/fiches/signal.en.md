# Signal memo sheets to print

Three **single A4 page** sheets taken from the [Signal groups](../guides/groupes-signal.md) guide: member, arrest or compromised group, organising roles.

!!! warning "Do not fill them in"

    These sheets contain **no names, numbers or places**: do not write any on them. Activist names and the fallback point are to be **memorised**, not written down. Avoid a shared workplace printer, which often keeps a print log.

<section class="memo__sheet" markdown="1">

## Sheet 1: member of a Signal group

- **Nothing illegal in writing**: no calls to violence or damage, no threats, even as a joke. Anything sensitive is said **in person, without a phone**.
- **No voice messages**: your voice identifies you. Write instead.
- **Links**: delete everything after the `?` before sharing.
- **Photos**: blur faces. No real names, addresses or screenshots.
- **No live location**: a meeting place is given in person.
- **Need help?** Name one person. **Emergency**: **15** or **112**, not the group.
- **A doubt about someone**: **HR**, in private. Never an accusation in the group.
- **Never share** the link or QR code of an airlock group, a trusted group or a sub-group. Only the public group's link is shared.
- **Memorise, without writing it down**: the activist name of your secretary or an HR member, and a **fallback point** agreed in person.
- **Role label**: only a protest role (medic, legal…), never an organising role.

</section>

<section class="memo__sheet" markdown="1">

## Sheet 2: arrested, phone lost, compromised group

**If you are arrested or lose your phone**

1. **In police custody, name nobody**: you have the right to remain silent, and nothing obliges you to talk about others or your groups.
2. **Tell HR** as soon as you can, in person or through a comrade: you will be removed from the groups as a precaution, nothing personal.
3. **Recover your account** on another phone (number and PIN), then delete unknown **linked devices**.
4. **At the debrief, say everything**: phone taken, unlocked, code given, time out of your sight. It is not a fault.

**If you are removed from a group**

- **Don't panic**: it is often a precaution.
- **Do not leave a group yourself** if it seems compromised: wait for instructions.
- **Do not create a replacement group**; go to the fallback point or contact HR.
- **Only accept an invitation from a keyholder or a secretary you know**; if in doubt, check in person.

**If the group is compromised**

- Someone is taken away or loses their phone → **precautionary removal, immediately**.
- Stranger in the group, leak, serious doubt → **"compromised group" procedure**: if you know where the leak is and it is removed, the group continues; otherwise, **you move out**.

</section>

<section class="memo__sheet" markdown="1">

## Sheet 3: organising roles

Roles **rotate**; nobody holds every role, and no group brings together all the key people.

| Role | Does | Does not |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Recruiter** | Talks about the movement, points to the public group and airlock groups | Vet; vouch for their own recruits; note anything about them |
| **Secretary** | Coordinates the meeting, **vets** when conditions are met, cleans the airlock group and adds HR | Vet without a voucher; **add** to the trusted group; keep an attendance list |
| **HR** | Second check, **add** to the trusted group, receive doubts, decide removals **in pairs**, {{ default("decision_delay") }} after a preventive pause | Run airlock groups; decide a removal alone |
| **Keyholder** | Watches over the group's settings (at least two per group), flags odd arrivals to HR | Add someone who has not been vetted; go on an action with their keyholder phone |
| **Moderation** | Reminds people of the charter, deals with issues in private | Accuse someone in the group |

</section>
