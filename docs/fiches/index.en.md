## Sheet 1: police custody

**A.M.I.S**: Avocat (lawyer), Médecin (doctor), Informer (a relative), Silence.

1. I ask for **a lawyer** and **a doctor**, from the start. Word for word: "Je veux voir un avocat.", "Je veux voir un médecin."
2. I give **my identity**, then **stay silent** until the lawyer arrives. "I have nothing to say" is a right.
3. I talk about **no one else**: no names, no descriptions.
4. I **re-read** every statement. I do not sign anything inaccurate; I can refuse to sign.
5. I **do not unlock** my phone without talking to the lawyer.
6. **Photos, fingerprints, DNA**: refusing is an offence (1 year and €15,000). I talk to **the lawyer before deciding**. Refusing the photo does not avoid the **TAJ** (criminal records processing file; guide "DNA, fingerprints and photos").
7. I note the **arrival time**, the officers' names, and what is taken from me.
8. **Minor**: my parents must be informed, a lawyer is compulsory.

**Length**: 24 hours, usually renewable once. **Rights**: tell a relative, see a doctor, have a lawyer.

## Sheet 2: first aid

**Protect** (myself, then the victim: not two victims) → **alert** (112 or 15, precise location) → **help**.

| Situation | I do |
| --- | --- |
| **Gas** | Move out **upwind**, without running. Do not rub. **Rinse** with saline. |
| **Heavy bleeding** | **Press hard** with a clean cloth, without letting go. |
| **Unconscious, breathing** | **Recovery position (PLS)**, **15**, watch breathing. |
| **Unconscious, not breathing** | **15**, **chest compressions** straight away, **without stopping**, **swap** every 2 min. |
| **Violent blow to head, neck, back** | **Do not move** them, hold head with both hands **in line**, 112. Helmet stays on. |
| **Eye hit** | **Do not rinse**, cover without pressing, 112. |
| **Burn** | Lukewarm water for 10 to 20 minutes. |
| **Grenade, explosion** | See a doctor; fragments are invisible on X-rays: ask for ultrasound or CT scan. |
| **Asthmatic** | Help them take **their own** inhaler, if they ask for it. |

**Never** give food or drink to a seriously injured or half-conscious person. **Do not** give medication (except the inhaler above).

**Afterwards**: have any injury **documented** by a doctor (certificate with ITT), photograph the injuries.
