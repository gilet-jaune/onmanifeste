# Democratic channels flouted: how we got here

A referendum bypassed, a citizens' convention emptied of its substance, laws passed without a vote, the coalition that came first set aside, a great debate with no follow-up, grievance books never published, a pensions conclave that led nowhere, a record petition debated without a vote, governments replaced within months. This timeline gathers the moments when **citizens' votes or voices were not acted upon**. They go a long way to explain distrust of institutions. If so many people take to the streets, it is not out of a taste for insurrection: it is often because the other channels led nowhere.

## When justice does not prosecute: what the figures say

Part of the distrust comes from the feeling that some cases, notably sexual violence and violence against minors, are not dealt with. What the sources establish, and what they do not:

- **Few prosecutions.** According to the Institut des politiques publiques, 83% of sexual-violence cases closed between 2012 and 2019 were not prosecuted, mostly because the offence was judged insufficiently established (56% of dismissals). The study stresses this does not mean the acts did not happen, and that defendants who are prosecuted are largely convicted.[^ipp-note-107-violences-sexuelles-2024]
- **Minors.** The head of the Ciivise (Commission indépendante sur l'inceste et les violences sexuelles faites aux enfants, a public body) considers that if 3% of those accused of raping minors are convicted, this is a "regime of impunity".[^publicsenat-ciivise-impunite] This is their analysis, not a court ruling.
- **The Epstein affair: an investigation did take place.** The Paris prosecutor opened a preliminary investigation on 23 August 2019 (rape, sexual assault, criminal conspiracy).[^euronews-epstein-enquete-2019] It led to one person being formally placed under investigation, who died in custody in 2022, and to a dismissal (non-lieu) in July 2023. On 14 February 2026, after millions of documents were released in the United States, the Paris prosecutor appointed lead magistrates to examine possible offences involving French nationals and announced a re-analysis of the file; the national financial prosecutor opened a preliminary investigation for aggravated laundering of tax fraud targeting a former minister, who has not been convicted.[^france24-epstein-parquet-2026] A proposal for a parliamentary inquiry was tabled on 5 February 2026; the Assembly's file shows no further step.[^an-dossier-epstein-2026]
- **What is not established.** None of the sources read states that a case was deliberately smothered or that members of government were protected. We name no one who has not been convicted: to file a complaint or report, see [filing a complaint](../guides/suites-judiciaires.md).
