# Pre-protest checklist

!!! abstract "Key points"

    - Get everything ready **the day before**: on site, the network saturates or can be cut.
    - Phone **charged**, power bank, locked with a **passcode**, apps and **offline** map installed and tested.
    - Useful numbers **on your skin** (under clothing) and on paper, without names.
    - A **buddy**, a **meeting point** and two routes home.
    - Nothing in your bag that could pass for a **weapon**.

Tick these off **at home, the day before or in the morning**. On site, the mobile network often saturates or can be cut: what isn't ready beforehand won't be ready during.

## Install this site offline

- [ ] Open this site once with a connection, then choose **"Add to Home Screen"** (or "Install app") in your phone's browser. It then works **without a network**. The footer shows the offline status and lets you check for updates.

## Phone

- [ ] **Charged to 100%**, with a **charged power bank** and its cable.
- [ ] Locked with a **passcode** (not fingerprint or face).[^eff-protest]
- [ ] **Apps installed and tested**: messaging ({{ res("signal") }}), messaging without Internet ({{ res("briar") }}), offline map. Add your Briar contacts now, together ([see "Communicating at a protest"](communiquer.md)).
- [ ] **Offline map** of the city downloaded (see below).
- [ ] **Automatic backup** of photos enabled on mobile data ([see "Filming the police"](filmer.md)).

## Offline map

Download the protest area **and** your way home. Test in airplane mode: the map and your position should show.

## On paper and on you

- [ ] Useful numbers written in permanent marker **on your skin under clothing** (upper arm, stomach — out of sight) and on paper: **only the 10 digits, with no name** (a close contact who is not protesting and answers hidden numbers, the legal team, a lawyer). In police custody, your phone and belongings are confiscated ([see "Never stay alone"](ne-pas-rester-seul.md)).[^sm-guide]
- [ ] A **trusted contact who holds your supporting documents** (photos of your ID, proof of address, work contract or student certificate): in case of immediate trial (comparution immédiate), they hand them to the lawyer ([proof of ties](suites-judiciaires.md)).
- [ ] The printed [pocket card](../carte/index.md).

## With your group

- [ ] A **buddy** and a **meeting point** away from the route, with a fallback time.
- [ ] An Android **comms lead** if the group relies on Briar.
- [ ] Two **routes home** ([see "Getting there and back"](venir-repartir.md)).

## In the bag

- [ ] ID, water, saline, a snack, medication: the full list is in ["Protest kit and outfit"](kit-manif.md).
- [ ] Nothing that could pass for a **weapon** ([see "Leave at home"](kit-manif.md#leave-at-home)).
