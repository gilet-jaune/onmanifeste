# Roles in a mobilisation

!!! abstract "Key points"

    - A mobilisation needs very different roles. **Nobody holds them all**, and they **rotate**.
    - The **Where** column says whether the role is played **on the ground**, **remotely** or **both**.
    - On-the-ground roles are shared **among the members already there**: you do not recruit for a role.
    - To come along without being in the heart of the march: [Protesting without being in the heart of the march](hors-du-coeur-du-cortege.md). To help from home: [Supporting without going to protests](soutenir-a-distance.md).

## The roles table

Protest roles operate according to your needs. They are shared **among the members already there**, according to each person's skills and availability, and can change from one action to the next. In an organised group, each on-the-ground role relies on a sub-group (`Medic`, `Legal`, `Veille`…): see [Signal groups](groupes-signal.md#protest-roles).

| Role | Where | What they do | What they don't do | Learn more |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Medic** | **On the ground** | First aid: gas, injuries, fainting | Medical procedures (stitching, removing fragments); giving medication | [First aid](premiers-secours.md) |
| **Legal** | **Both** | Observes arrests (on the ground); lawyer contacts, phone line and follow-up of people arrested (possible remotely) | Replace a lawyer | [Police custody](garde-a-vue.md) |
| **Field watch** | **On the ground** | Reports, from public space, where gas is being fired, where a line is advancing or pulling back, which streets are closed and which way out is clear; short factual messages ("gas on X street, exit clear on Y side") in a `Veille` sub-group | Point out, photograph or describe an officer; urge people to go towards a line or round it to confront it; note down people; act outside a declared demonstration | [Dispersal, warnings and kettling](dispersion.md) · [First aid](premiers-secours.md) |
| **Protection and de-escalation** | **On the ground** | Spots tension, talks, helps people out of a kettle, accompanies vulnerable people | Carry anything that could pass for a weapon; respond to provocation | [Dispersal, warnings and kettling](dispersion.md) · [Kit and what to wear](kit-manif.md) |
| **Documentation** | **On the ground** | Films arrests and violence, keeps the evidence | Publish without blurring protesters | [Film the police](filmer.md) |
| **Support** | **Both** | Listens and accompanies after a hard moment | Play therapist | [Mental health](sante-mentale.md) |
| **Logistics** | **Both** | Transport, food, water, storage | Store equipment that could pass for a weapon | [Getting there and back](venir-repartir.md) |
| **Build** | **Both** | Banners, placards, equipment | Equipment that could pass for a weapon | [Signs, banners, flags and leaflets](faire-sa-pancarte.md) · [Kit and what to wear](kit-manif.md) |
| **Communication** | **Both** | Visuals, announcements, social media | Repost far-right content | [Communicating at a protest](communiquer.md) · [Social media](reseaux-sociaux.md) |
| **Relay and contact person** | **Remote** | Circulates calls from legal teams and funds; answers the phone, alerts the legal team, meets people leaving custody | Share names or photos of people; call the police instead of the legal team | [Supporting without going to protests](soutenir-a-distance.md#be-the-contact-person-outside) |
| **Funding and supplies** | **Remote** | Gives to a solidarity fund; equips street medics with new, in-date supplies | Transfer money to a person rather than a fund; hand supplies to someone they do not know | [Solidarity fund](soutenir-a-distance.md#give-to-a-solidarity-fund) · [Street medics](soutenir-a-distance.md#equip-street-medics) |
| **Temporary team coordination** | **Both** | Sets up a disposable Signal group for a specific task (kitchen, transport, welcome) and closes it at the end | Put names, addresses or photos in it; link it to trusted groups | [Coordinate for a specific task](soutenir-a-distance.md#coordinate-for-a-specific-task) · [Signal groups](groupes-signal.md) |
| **Inform and persuade** | **Remote** | Shares checked information, debunks false claims, talks to relatives | Relay without checking | [Checking information](desinformation.md) · [Convince your relatives](convaincre-ses-proches.md) |
| **Civic voice** | **Remote** | Votes, signs petitions, writes to elected officials, gets trained | Sign without reading the platform's terms (personal data) | [Vote](soutenir-a-distance.md#vote) · [Petitions](soutenir-a-distance.md#sign-petitions) |

Blockades and civil disobedience carry criminal risks: see [what the law forbids at protests](risques-penaux.md).

**Field watch: the framework.** This role is for **getting out in time and avoiding gas**, not for confrontation. In the texts we read, we found nothing banning the reporting of a deployment's position from public space; that is not a guarantee. Two limits:

- knowingly taking part in a group that is preparing violence or damage is punishable by one year in prison and a €15,000 fine,[^cp-222-14-2] and written messages serve as evidence: the same message can read differently in a hostile context;
- sharing information that identifies or locates a person in order to expose them to a risk is punishable by 3 years in prison and a €45,000 fine, or 5 years and €75,000 if the person holds public authority.[^cp-223-1-1]

So report **zones and directions**, never **people**. Use a dedicated sub-group with disappearing messages.

## Where this list comes from

The "on the ground" roles are those described for organised groups. The "remote" roles are ours: they are inspired by the idea of mapping roles in a social movement, popularised by Deepa Iyer's "Social Change Ecosystem Map" (Building Movement Project, 2017).[^bmp-ecosystem-map] We reuse neither its labels nor its definitions: the page states no reuse licence.
