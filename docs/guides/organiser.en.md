# Organising a protest

!!! abstract "Key points"

    - You **declare** at the town hall or prefecture, **3 to 15 full days** beforehand.
    - Undeclared or banned demonstration: the organiser risks **6 months and €7,500**.
    - Plan stewards, **street medics**, a **legal team** and an accessible point.
    - Afterwards: review with the legal team (arrests, injuries, videos).

Organising means notifying, preparing safety and planning what comes after. The obligations fall mainly on the **organisers**.

!!! tip "Where to start?"

    - Notifying the protest: [notify](#notify).
    - Preparing security: [prepare](#prepare).
    - Afterwards: [afterwards](#afterwards).
    - Organising away from city centres: [organising locally](#organising-locally-beyond-city-centres).

## Notify

- In France, a protest on the public highway is **notified**; it does not need to be authorised.[^amnesty-droits]
- Notification is made to the **town hall** or **prefecture**, at least **3 clear days** and at most **15 clear days** before the date. Some cities, including Paris, ask for longer: check the official page.[^sp-organiser]
- It includes the organisers' names and contact details, purpose, place, date, time, route and expected turnout. You receive a receipt.[^sp-organiser]

!!! warning "Risks for organisers"

    Organising a **non-notified** or **banned** protest, or filing an **inaccurate** notification to mislead about its purpose or conditions, is punishable by 6 months in prison and a €7,500 fine. This offence targets organisers, not ordinary participants.[^cdj-interdiction]

## Prepare

- [ ] Identifiable **stewards**, trained in de-escalation and in spotting exits.
- [ ] **Street medics** and a **legal team** on board, with their numbers shared with participants.
- [ ] An **accessible point** and an "accessibility" contact for disabled people.
- [ ] An encrypted information channel ([Signal](../annuaire/index.md#signal)) for live instructions; to structure your groups while limiting leaks of personal data: [Signal groups](groupes-signal.md).
- [ ] Encrypted shared documents ({{ res("cryptpad") }}) for contact lists, the plan and roles, rather than a mainstream online service.
- [ ] A clear **dispersal** point and instructions in case of warnings ([dispersal and warnings](dispersion.md)).

## Afterwards

- Debrief with the legal team: arrests, injuries, testimonies and videos to gather.

## Organising locally, beyond city centres

- **Why not rely solely on city-centre marches or school blockades**: crowds concentrated in the same city-centre streets every Saturday are more often exposed to kettling and tear gas, while a few clashes are enough for rolling news channels to overshadow demands and justify curfews and bans.[^wiki-17-octobre-2026][^amnesty-surenchere-2026] Likewise, an isolated high-school blockade does not halt the economy and exposes young people to mass repression (over 5,000 arrests in autumn 2026).[^amnesty-lyceens-2026] Social history (1936, 1968, 1995, the Yellow Vests in 2018) shows that leverage grows when a movement **broadens to other groups and key sectors** (transport, energy, logistics, education) and **puts down local roots** in small towns, neighbourhoods and rural areas.
- [ ] **Make support visible every day**: place a yellow vest on your dashboard, display support in a window or on a balcony, and talk with others who do the same.
- [ ] **Create friendly local meeting points near home**: local markets, town squares, workplace gates or **welcoming roundabouts** (shared meals, banners, conversations) let residents put off by city-centre clashes meet and organise on a human scale.[^wiki-17-octobre-2026]
- [ ] **Agree on the next meeting in person before leaving**: if social networks are saturated, throttled by algorithms or administratively blocked, a recurring physical meeting point (local assembly, roundabout, market) keeps the group going without relying on screens.[^blast-coupure-reseaux-2026]
- [ ] **Support strikes in strategic sectors**: join information pickets and contribute to strike funds so workers in transport, energy or public services can hold out ([strikes, protests and work](travail.md)).
- [ ] **Never post an invite link to an organising Signal group on Facebook or public social media**: police and the far right join open links immediately. Use public channels only to announce open gatherings, then create small {{ res("signal") }} groups with people you have met in person, hiding your phone number (Signal username) and enabling disappearing messages.[^eff-signal]
- **Roundabouts and traffic**: gathering peacefully on the verge of a roundabout, holding a banner or handing leaflets to stopped motorists without blocking the lane falls under freedom of expression.[^amnesty-droits] By contrast, **obstructing or blocking traffic** on a public road is a criminal offence punishable by **2 years in prison and a €4,500 fine**, as well as driving-licence suspension.[^cdr-l412-1]
