# Film the police

**Film police interventions, always.** It is legal in France: officers on duty have no special right over their image, and the freedom to inform prevails.[^amnesty-filmer][^ldh-filmer] This footage is often the only evidence of violence or of an inaccurate police report; France's Défenseure des droits calls it "very useful".[^ddd-videos]

But footage can also expose protesters. Film the police, protect the people.

## Before: automatic backup

If your phone is seized, broken or stolen, footage that wasn't backed up is lost.

- [ ] Turn on **automatic backup** of photos and videos, **including over mobile data**: by default it often only runs on Wi-Fi.[^icloud-cellular][^google-backup]
- [ ] Choose an **end-to-end encrypted** service (Ente, Proton Drive, or iCloud with "Advanced Data Protection"): otherwise the provider can access your images and may be required to hand them over.[^apple-adp][^icloud-security]
- [ ] Before the protest, check that a test photo shows up in the cloud **over 4G/5G**, with Wi-Fi off.

## During

- [ ] Start **before** the action if you can, and film **continuously**: what comes before and after matters as much as the act itself.[^witness-preuve]
- [ ] Show the **context**: a street sign, a landmark, the time on screen or said out loud.[^witness-preuve]
- [ ] **Frame the officers**: actions, weapons used, unit, ID number (RIO) if visible. Avoid the faces of protesters and of people being arrested.[^witness-police]
- [ ] Hold the phone **horizontally** and steady; keep your distance, don't obstruct the intervention.
- [ ] Don't stop filming if told to without reason: it is your right. Stay calm and polite.[^amnesty-filmer]

## Right after: keep the evidence

- [ ] **Never edit the original** (no cropping, no filter): its metadata (date, place, device) is what makes it evidence.[^witness-metadata]
- [ ] **Get a copy off the phone** as soon as possible: send it to the legal team, a lawyer, or encrypted storage. A phone can be seized or broken.[^witness-archivage]
- [ ] Write down the time, exact place and what happened before the recording started.
- [ ] An app like **ProofMode** adds verification data proving the file has not been altered.[^proofmode]

## Publish without exposing

- [ ] Publish a **copy**, never the original.
- [ ] **Blur everything that makes a person identifiable**, not only faces: tattoos, haircut or hair colour, recognisable clothes, bags or shoes, badges, banners bearing a name, voices (mute the sound if needed). Signal's blur tool helps with faces; the rest is done by hand.[^signal-blur][^witness-police][^eff-protest]
- [ ] **Strip the metadata** from the copy (mat2): it reveals your location and device.[^mat2]
- [ ] **Archive the post** (yours, or an official statement) with the Wayback Machine or archive.today: it stays available if deleted.[^wayback] Never upload raw footage showing faces to a public archive.

## Pass it on

- To the **legal team** and the arrested person's lawyer: footage can change the outcome of a trial.
- To the **Défenseur des droits** for misconduct by security forces.[^ddd]
- To a media outlet or an observatory (LDH) if you want to document publicly.
