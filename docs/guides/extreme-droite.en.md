# Far-right attacks

!!! abstract "Key points"

    - Attacks often happen **after dispersal**: come and leave **as a group**.
    - Badges and flags **in your bag** on the way.
    - Don't respond to provocation; if attacked, move towards the crowd and call **17** or **112**.
    - Afterwards: medical certificate, witnesses, videos, **complaint**; write down the exact racist or homophobic insults.

Violent far-right groups attack marches or isolated protesters, often **after dispersal**. In October 2026, violence was reported on the sidelines of protests in around fifteen French cities.[^franceinfo][^lessentiel] This guide is about **not becoming a target** and reacting if it happens, not about seeking confrontation.

## Before

- [ ] Ask the organisers and the legal team whether groups are expected in your city.
- [ ] Come **as a group** and plan to go back together ([never stay alone](ne-pas-rester-seul.md), [getting there and back](venir-repartir.md)).
- [ ] Keep badges, stickers and flags **in your bag** on the way: they make you identifiable once away from the march.

## During

- Stay **in the march**, near the stewards; tell them about any masked or hostile group.
- **Don't give chase** and don't respond to provocation: you risk injury and prosecution.
- If you film, do it **from a distance** and with others ([film the police](filmer.md)).

## If you are attacked

1. **Get away** towards the crowd, an open shop, a station; protect your head.
2. **Shout** to draw attention, call **17** or **112**.
3. Get treated by street medics or A&E ([first aid](premiers-secours.md)).

## Afterwards

- Have your injuries recorded by a **doctor** (certificate with ITT) and gather **videos and witnesses**.
- **File a complaint**. Violence committed **as a group**, **with a weapon** or with **racist, antisemitic or homophobic** insults is punished more severely: write down the exact words used.[^aci][^justifit]
- If the police did not intervene, note the time, place and unit present, and refer it to the {{ res("defenseur-des-droits") }}.
- Associations can support you: {{ res("licra") }}, {{ res("sos-racisme") }}, {{ res("sos-homophobie") }}, and {{ res("france-victimes", "France Victimes") }} on **116 006**.
- Online threats or calls to violence: report them on {{ res("pharos", "PHAROS") }} ([online harassment](violences-harcelement.md)).

Several far-right groups have been **dissolved** for their violence, such as the GUD in 2024.[^dissolution]
