# Sexist violence and online harassment

!!! abstract "Key points"

    - At a protest: stay with your **buddy** and agree on a signal.
    - Sexist or sexual violence: **3919**, free and anonymous, 24/7.
    - Online harassment: **don't reply**, take dated screenshots, report (platform, Pharos).
    - Cyberbullying: **3018**, which also helps get content removed.

Crowds, chaos and exposure on social media create specific risks. You are not alone: free helplines exist.

## At the protest

- Stay with your **buddy** and agree on a signal to step away ([never stay alone](ne-pas-rester-seul.md)).
- If someone assaults or gropes you: move away, alert your group, the stewards or street medics.
- Afterwards: you can have it recorded by a doctor, file a complaint, and call **3919**, a free anonymous 24/7 line for victims of sexist and sexual violence.[^3919]

## Online: harassment and doxxing

Posting your protest photos can expose you to harassment or to your personal details being published ("doxxing").

- [ ] **Don't reply**: keep **screenshots** showing the page address and the date.
- [ ] **Report** the content to the platform, then on {{ res("pharos", "PHAROS") }} if it is illegal (threats, calls to violence).[^pharos]
- [ ] Call **3018**, the free national number against cyberharassment, which also helps get content removed.[^3018]
- [ ] Request **removal** of your personal data published online.[^cnil]
- Publishing someone's private information to expose them to a risk is an offence in France, punishable by 3 years in prison and a €45,000 fine.[^doxxing]
