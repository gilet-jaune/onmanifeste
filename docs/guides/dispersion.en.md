# Dispersal, warnings and kettling

Recognising a dispersal order, and leaving in time, avoids arrest: staying after the warnings can become an offence.

## What the law says

- An **unlawful assembly** (attroupement) is a gathering on the public highway likely to disturb public order. It can be dispersed by force after **two warnings** (sommations) go unheeded.[^ldh-dispersion][^avocat-attroupement]
- **Voluntarily staying** in an unlawful assembly after the warnings carries up to one year in prison and a €15,000 fine; up to 3 years and €45,000 with a covered face.[^avocat-attroupement]
- Taking part in a protest that was simply **not notified** is not an offence; taking part in a **banned** protest carries a €135 fine.[^amnesty-droits]

## Recognising the warnings

- They are announced by the police, often by **loudspeaker** ("Obéissance à la loi, dispersez-vous"), before force is used.[^ldh-dispersion]
- From the **first warning**, start leaving: the second can follow quickly, with gas or a charge right after.

## Leaving safely

- [ ] Spot the exit streets and your group's meeting point **beforehand** ([never stay alone](ne-pas-rester-seul.md)).
- [ ] Leave **calmly**, as a group, without running into the crowd; move away from the police line.
- [ ] Keep your hands visible and don't turn back "to see what is happening".
- [ ] If every exit is blocked, **say so** to the officers and ask where to go; staying because you cannot leave is not staying "voluntarily". Note the time and situation, film if you can.[^avocat-attroupement]

## If you are kettled

**Kettling** (encerclement, "nasse") means boxing in a group of protesters. In 2021, the Conseil d'État annulled the rules framing it in the national policing scheme, for lack of sufficient safeguards.[^ce-snmo][^lemonde-droit-snmo]

- Stay **calm and together**, away from the edges where people push.
- Ask officers for **a way out**; point out anyone ill, underage or in distress.
- **Film** and note the time, place and duration: it matters if the situation is challenged later ([film the police](filmer.md)).
- Alert the **legal team** if checks or arrests are announced at the exit.
