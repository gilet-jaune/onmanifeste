# Police custody (garde à vue)

!!! abstract "Key points"

    - Ask **straight away for a lawyer and a doctor**.
    - **Stay silent** until you have spoken with the lawyer; **never talk about others**.
    - **Read** every statement; don't sign anything inaccurate.
    - Length: **24 hours**, renewable once.
    - On release: write everything down while it's fresh, keep the documents, contact a **legal team**.

!!! warning "Disclaimer"

    General information about French law, not legal advice. Your lawyer is the only person to trust with the details of your situation.

## Flowchart: from arrest to release

<div class="flow" markdown>

1. **What are you told?**
    - **ID check** (no custody) → **4 hours maximum**; you can have the prosecutor and a person of your choice informed.[^sp-retenue]
    - **Police custody** → follow the steps below.
2. **As soon as your rights are read** → ask for **a lawyer and a doctor**, have a relative informed.[^sp-gav]
3. **Are you a minor?**
    - **Yes** → a lawyer is **mandatory**, parents are informed, interviews are filmed.[^sp-mineur]
    - **No** → 24 hours, renewable once.[^sp-gav]
4. **During interviews** → **stay silent** until you have spoken with the lawyer; **never talk about others**.[^sp-gav]
5. **A statement to sign?**
    - **Accurate** → you can sign.
    - **Inaccurate** → don't sign, or have it corrected.
6. **On release** → write everything down while it's fresh, keep the documents; fast-track trial: you can **ask for more time**.[^sp-ci] Contact a legal team.

</div>

## Your rights, from the start

They must be notified to you immediately, in a language you understand:[^sp-gav]

- **The right to remain silent.** You can give your identity and refuse to answer other questions.
- **A lawyer**, of your choice or court-appointed (free depending on your income). Since 1 July 2024, you can ask that no questioning takes place without a lawyer.[^sp-gav-2024]
- **A doctor.**
- **Have someone notified**: a relative and your employer. The police make the call (often from a hidden number), which is why you should write the **10 digits of a close contact on your skin under clothing** before the protest: since your phone is taken during the search, never ask to unlock it in front of officers to look up a number.[^sp-gav][^sm-guide]
- **An interpreter** if needed.
- Access to some case documents (notification report, medical certificate).

Duration: **24 hours**, renewable once (48 hours) for most offences alleged at protests.[^sp-gav]

## Reflexes

1. **Ask for a lawyer** and a doctor as soon as your rights are read.
2. **Say nothing** before speaking to the lawyer, even "to explain yourself": anything you say can be used. Saying "I have nothing to declare" is a right.
3. **Don't talk about others**: no names, no descriptions, no "who was there".
4. **Re-read** every statement (procès-verbal). Don't sign anything inaccurate; you can refuse to sign or ask for corrections.
5. Stay calm: provocation and pressure happen, don't let yourself be pushed into a mistake.

!!! warning "Fingerprints, DNA, phone code"

    Refusing fingerprints, photos or DNA sampling is an offence in itself. Refusing to give your phone unlock code can also be one when it is requested by a formal judicial order (Cour de cassation, 7 November 2022). **Ask your lawyer** before deciding.[^cpp-55-1][^cpp-706-56][^cp-434-15-2][^cass-2022]

## Minors

- **Parents** (or legal guardians) must be notified.[^sp-mineur]
- A **lawyer is mandatory**; a **medical examination** is provided.
- Interviews are video-recorded.[^sp-mineur]
- Under 13, no custody (only a strictly supervised "retenue").[^sp-mineur]

## On release

- Write down **straight away** what happened: times, places, words, names or badge numbers, conditions of detention.
- Keep **all documents** given to you (summons, warning, notice of trial).
- **Fast-track trial (comparution immédiate)**: you can ask for **more time** to prepare your defence.[^sp-ci]
- Contact a **legal team** or a local anti-repression collective ({{ res("rajcol", "list of local collectives") }}) for follow-up, and a **solidarity fund** for costs.[^legal-team]
- Lawyer's fees: you may be entitled to {{ res("sp-aide-juridictionnelle", "legal aid") }} depending on your income.[^sp-aj]
- Violence or misconduct by the police: [file a complaint and report it](suites-judiciaires.md).
