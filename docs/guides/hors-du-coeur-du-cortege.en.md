# Protesting without being in the heart of the march

!!! abstract "Key points"

    - You can take part **without being at the front or in the middle**: at the back or the sides, near exits, in a calm gathering, or from home.
    - Choose a place from which you can **leave easily**, and **leave early**.
    - For most of these choices the site cites **no rule of law**: they are organisational pointers, gathered here from the other guides.

This page gathers advice found in other guides, for people who are fragile, disabled, older, foreign with a fragile situation, with a child, or simply afraid.

## Choosing your place in a march

| Your situation | What is advised | Where to read |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Asthma, respiratory failure, heart problem | Stay **at the back or on the ventilated sides** of the march, inhaler within reach, filtering mask.[^allodocteurs-proteger][^medic-gestes] | [Disability or chronic condition](handicap.md) |
| Reduced mobility, wheelchair | Stay near **clear perpendicular streets** (no barriers or high kerbs) to leave at the first signs of tension.[^reporterre-handicap] | [Disability or chronic condition](handicap.md) |
| Sensory hypersensitivity, tinnitus | Earplugs or noise-cancelling headphones, and an **agreed sign** with your buddy to leave without speaking.[^reporterre-handicap] | [Disability or chronic condition](handicap.md) |
| Fragile situation (no papers, ongoing procedure) | A **less exposed** spot in the march and leaving **before the dispersal**. | [Foreign national](etrangers.md) |
| With a child or an animal | Two adults if possible, a meeting point, leaving **at the first signs of tension**. | [Before you go](avant-de-partir.md#bringing-a-child-or-an-animal) |
| Afraid of tense marches | A **friendly roundabout** or a **local market**, or a strike fund. | [Convince your relatives](convaincre-ses-proches.md) |

In every case, stay in a part of the march where you are comfortable, spot the **exits** and side streets, and do not stay alone: see [Never stay alone](ne-pas-rester-seul.md).

## Leave in time

- Leave **before** the warnings if you can: [leaving safely](dispersion.md#leaving-safely).
- If every exit is blocked: see [dispersal and kettling](dispersion.md).
- Plan your return in advance: [getting there and back](venir-repartir.md#getting-back).

## Calm gatherings

A roundabout, a market or a fixed point can replace the march. What is allowed and what is punished (blocking traffic) is detailed in [organising locally](organiser.md#organising-locally-beyond-city-centres).

## If you cannot or do not want to go

You can help without being there: money, supplies, food, transport, online relay, voting, petitions. See [Supporting without going to protests](soutenir-a-distance.md).

## What this guide does not say

We found **no official source** on the best place in a march: the pointers above come from health and organisation guides, not from legal texts. Ask a legal team or a local collective for advice.
