# The Palestinian flag at protests

!!! abstract "Key points"

    - Protesting in support of the Palestinian population is **legal**.
    - A ban must be **justified case by case**, not just by the cause defended.
    - Banned demonstration: **€135** fine, no police custody for that fine alone.
    - Placards and posts: **glorifying terrorism** is a heavily punished offence; write clearly what you stand for.

Demonstrating in support of the Palestinian population is legal. What you can be blamed for depends on the **setting** (banned demonstration or not) and on **what your placards, slogans and posts say**, not on the flag itself.

## What the courts say

- A demonstration cannot be banned **solely because it supports the Palestinian population**, nor solely on the basis of a minister's instruction: the prefect must show, case by case, a concrete risk to public order, under the judge's review.[^ce-palestine-2023]

## If the demonstration is banned

- [ ] **Check before you go**: a ban order is published by the prefecture (official bulletin, website, social media).[^ldh-manif-interdite]
- Taking part in a demonstration **banned by order** is a petty offence: a fixed fine of **€135**, up to €750 before a court. Demonstrating without prior notice, on the other hand, is still the exercise of a freedom.[^ldh-manif-interdite]
- For a petty offence alone, you can be **neither arrested nor held in police custody**. You can contest the fine and argue the ban order was unlawful.[^ldh-manif-interdite]
- Example: on 12 October 2023 in Paris, the police prefecture announced **3,000 fines** of €135.[^politis-palestine-2025]

## Placards, slogans and posts

To make and carry a sign, banner or flag, see [Signs, banners, flags and leaflets](faire-sa-pancarte.md).

- **Glorifying terrorism** (presenting a terrorist act or its perpetrators favourably) is an offence punishable by 5 years in prison and a €75,000 fine, 7 years and €100,000 when done online.[^cdj-apologie-terrorisme]
- Prosecutions have risen sharply: **452 judgments and 305 convictions** for glorifying terrorism between October 2023 and November 2024.[^politis-palestine-2025]
- [ ] Write **clearly what you stand for** (ceasefire, human rights, international law) and avoid any slogan or symbol that could be read as support for a terrorist act: that is what prosecutions rest on, not the flag.
- [ ] What you post online can be used against you, even months later.

## On town halls and public buildings

The rules differ for an **institution**: in the name of the **neutrality of public services**, an administrative judge can order a town hall to take a flag down, as in Montataire on 22 September 2025, with a daily penalty.[^ta-amiens-montataire-2025] This does not apply to demonstrators.
