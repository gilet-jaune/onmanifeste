# High-school students: blockades, absences, sanctions

High-school students have the right to protest. But school has its own rules, on top of those of the street.

## What is allowed, what is not

- **Protesting**: yes, it is a right, as long as it does not harm teaching activities.[^cidj]
- **Striking**: no, students have no right to strike. Missing class to protest is not an excused absence.[^cidj]
- **Blockading the school**: not allowed. Preventing students or staff from entering can be a criminal obstruction offence, on top of disciplinary sanctions.[^cidj][^avocat-blocus]

## Possible sanctions

The list of sanctions is set by the Education Code: warning, reprimand, accountability measure, temporary exclusion of up to eight days, permanent exclusion. The heaviest go through a **disciplinary board** (conseil de discipline).[^avocat-blocus]

- You must be **told what you are accused of** and be able to present your defence.[^avocat-blocus]
- Keep the **summonses** and write down what happened; ask a students' union or association for advice.

## If you are arrested

Minors have reinforced safeguards in custody: parents notified, mandatory lawyer, medical examination.[^sp-mineur]
