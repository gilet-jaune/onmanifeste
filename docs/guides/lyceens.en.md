# High-school students: blockades, absences, sanctions

High-school students have the right to protest. But school has its own rules, on top of those of the street.

## What is allowed, what is not

- **Protesting**: yes, it is a right, as long as it does not harm teaching activities.[^cidj]
- **Striking**: no, students have no right to strike. Missing class to protest is not an excused absence.[^cidj]
- **Blockading the school**: not allowed. Preventing students or staff from entering can be a criminal obstruction offence, on top of disciplinary sanctions.[^cidj][^avocat-blocus]

## Around school: staying safe

In front of the school, on the public road, the rules of any demonstration apply, and the police can step in.

- [ ] **Never stay alone**: come and leave as a group ([never stay alone](ne-pas-rester-seul.md)).
- [ ] Carry the **number of a trusted adult** and of a legal team, written on paper ([pre-protest checklist](avant-de-partir.md)).
- [ ] If the police step in, move away and **never** pick up a grenade, even one lying on the ground: demonstrators have lost a hand that way.[^publicsenat-gli-f4-retrait] See [dispersal, warnings and kettling](dispersion.md).
- [ ] If you witness an arrest or violence: film from a distance, without showing students' faces ([film the police](filmer.md)).

## Possible sanctions

The list of sanctions is set by the Education Code: warning, reprimand, accountability measure, temporary exclusion of up to eight days, permanent exclusion.[^avocat-blocus] The head teacher can impose most of them alone; permanent exclusion goes through a **disciplinary council**.[^cidj-sanctions]

- You must be **told what you are accused of** and be able to defend yourself.[^avocat-blocus]
- Keep the **letters** you receive and write down what happened; ask an association or a high-school students' union for advice.

### The disciplinary council

- [ ] You and your parents must be **summoned by registered letter at least 8 days in advance**.[^cidj-sanctions]
- [ ] You can **read your file** before the hearing.[^cidj-sanctions]
- [ ] You can be **assisted** by a lawyer, a teacher or a student.[^cidj-sanctions]
- [ ] To challenge the decision, **appeal to the rector within 8 days** of receiving the letter.[^cidj-sanctions]
- Sanctions do not follow you through school: warnings, reprimands and accountability measures are removed from your file at the end of the school year, other sanctions (except permanent exclusion) after one year.[^cidj-sanctions]

## If you are arrested

From the age of 13, a minor can be held in police custody, with stronger safeguards:[^sp-mineur]

- a **lawyer is mandatory** from the start;
- your **parents** (or legal guardians) must be **informed**, normally within 3 hours;
- a **medical examination** is mandatory from the start;
- **every interview is filmed**.

You have the right to remain silent. Don't sign anything you don't understand ([the police custody guide](garde-a-vue.md)).
