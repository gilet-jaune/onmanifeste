# Protest songs

!!! abstract "In short"

    - Singing together **holds a march together** and gives courage: a simple refrain is enough.
    - Lyrics are **not copied** in this guide: many recent texts are protected by copyright. Each song links to the page that publishes it.
    - **Old, anonymous** songs circulate freely; **recent** texts (parodies, translations, arrangements) have authors who keep their rights.
    - A song that **names people** can lead to a defamation complaint: sing about facts, not names.

This guide does not list every protest song. It presents a few, taken from the collection of an activist brass band[^fibzh-chants], which publishes full lyrics and playing notes. The page gives neither authors nor a licence: **check the rights before reproducing any text**. The lyrics are in French, Italian or Spanish.

## Old or traditional songs

- **Bella ciao**: Italian song, partisan words fixed around 1944-1945 on an older, anonymous melody.[^wp-bella-ciao] [Lyrics and score](https://fibzh.org/chants/#be).
- **La Lega**: struggle song of farm women workers in the Po plain, late 19th century. [Lyrics](https://fibzh.org/chants/#la).
- **A las barricadas**: words by Valeriano Orobón Fernández (1933), anthem of the Spanish CNT, to the tune of the _Varsovienne_.[^wp-barricadas] [Lyrics](https://fibzh.org/chants/#al).
- **La Semaine sanglante**: song about the repression of the Paris Commune in 1871. [Lyrics](https://fibzh.org/chants/#ss). I could not confirm the author or their date of death.

## Recent songs

- **Hymne des femmes**: created collectively by MLF activists in March 1971, to the tune of the _Chant des marais_.[^wp-mlf] [Lyrics](https://fibzh.org/chants/#hf).
- **L'Estaca** and **El Pueblo**: two classics of the protest repertoire, whose authors or rights holders keep their rights. [L'Estaca (French version, waltz)](https://fibzh.org/chants/#es), [El Pueblo](https://fibzh.org/chants/#el).
- **J'ai 2 passions**: [lyrics](https://fibzh.org/chants/#2p).
- **Bella thalasso**: parody of _Bella ciao_ about a local project. [Lyrics](https://fibzh.org/chants/#th).
- **On n'en veut pas !**: about the pension reform. [Lyrics](https://fibzh.org/chants/#oe).
- **La révolte des sardinières**: [lyrics](https://fibzh.org/chants/#rs).
- **À bas l'État policier**: [lyrics](https://fibzh.org/chants/#et). The text names victims of police violence; read it again before singing it.

## Singing without exposing yourself

- **A short refrain** is remembered at once and taken up without paper.
- **No names of police officers or officials**: a sung insult or accusation is still an insult or an accusation.
- **A refrain learned by heart** avoids waving a lyric sheet.
