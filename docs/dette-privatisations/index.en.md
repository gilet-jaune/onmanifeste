# Debt, privatisations and public money

This page brings together, under one timeline, the milestones on the State's debt, how it finances itself, the privatisations of public companies, public spending (consulting firms, purchasing bodies) and tax evasion. It does not say whether these choices were right: it dates what happened, with the source of every fact.

!!! info "How to read this page"

    - **Established facts**: a law, a ruling of the Constitutional Council or of an authority, a Senate report, an INSEE figure.
    - **Readings and investigations**: a study, a report or an NGO's work is not a decision. Where it is recorded, each event's nature is shown under its theme.
    - **Disagreements**: when official sources contradict each other (for example on motorway profitability), both readings appear in the timeline.
    - **People**: we name no one who has not been convicted.
