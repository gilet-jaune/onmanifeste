# Documented repression and disinformation

Violence committed during policing of protests, and official statements later contradicted by the facts.

!!! info "Our rules and limitations"

    - **Established facts only**: court ruling, official report or inquiry, authenticated video, official recognition or correction. Each entry links to its **evidence**; no one who has not been convicted is named.
    - **Known biases and limitations**: this selection is **not exhaustive**. It depends on cases where independent evidence (video, witness, investigative journalism) existed and became public, and court proceedings often take several years. Something missing or wrong? [Report it]({{ repositories.docs.url }}/issues/new/choose).
