# Injured and killed at protests

The specialised databases that track individual cases over the years, and a timeline of **major historical milestones and movement-wide tolls** (1891 to the present).

!!! info "Our rules and limitations"

    - **No duplication of specialised databases**: ongoing case-by-case tracking of victims and court proceedings is carried out by the newsrooms and collectives listed below ({{ res("basta-violences-policieres", "Basta!") }}, {{ res("desarmons-les") }}, {{ res("mutiles-pour-l-exemple", "Mutilés pour l'exemple") }}, {{ res("allo-place-beauvau", "Allô Place Beauvau") }}). Our timeline focuses on **major historical milestones and movement-wide tolls**.
    - Every entry links to its **sources**: court ruling, inquiry, recognised census or investigative outlet.
    - Out of respect, only **deceased** victims or cases that are already widely public are named; injured people appear as **tallies**. No living minor is named.
    - **Known biases and limitations**: in the absence of complete official statistics, this timeline relies on cases reported to collectives or covered by the press and is **not exhaustive**. Judicial outcomes often take years, and many complaints are dismissed or end in a non-suit when the individual officer cannot be identified.
    - No needlessly graphic detail. A mistake, something missing? [Report it]({{ repositories.docs.url }}/issues/new/choose).
